HAT-P-67b

Inflated hot Saturn · 1,200 light-years

How big is HAT-P-67b?

HAT-P-67b is about 299,200 km across.

See HAT-P-67b in 3D, at true scale

HAT-P-67b is one of the largest planets known and one of the least dense — a Saturn-mass world blown up to more than twice Jupiter's width, trailing a vast cloud of escaping helium.

Good to know

  • In 2023 astronomers measured helium streaming away from it in enormous tails.
  • It is losing hundreds of thousands of tonnes of gas every second.
  • Its density is roughly that of candy floss.
