Io

Moon of Jupiter · 778 million km from the Sun

How big is Io?

Io is about 3,643 km across.

See Io in 3D, at true scale

Io is squeezed between Jupiter's gravity and the pull of the other large moons, and the friction melts its insides. The result is the most volcanically active world known: hundreds of erupting volcanoes, lava lakes, and plumes that rise hundreds of kilometres.

Good to know

  • Its surface is repaved so fast that it has essentially no impact craters.
  • The yellows, oranges and whites are sulfur and sulfur dioxide frost.
  • Loki Patera, a lava lake 200 km wide, brightens on a roughly 500-day cycle.
