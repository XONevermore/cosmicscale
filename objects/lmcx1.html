LMC X-1

Black hole in another galaxy · About 160,000 light-years

How big is LMC X-1?

LMC X-1 is about 64.4 km across.

See LMC X-1 in 3D, at true scale

LMC X-1 is a black hole in the Large Magellanic Cloud, a small galaxy orbiting ours. It pulls gas off a huge young giant star, and the gas glows in X-rays. This view is an illustration.

At a glance

Diameter64.4 km
Mass2.17 × 10³¹ kg
Diameter compared with Earth0.005 × Earth
CompanionAbout 32 Suns
Orbit3.9 days
DistanceAbout 160,000 light-years

Good to know

  • Its companion is about three times heavier than the black hole.
  • The two circle each other every 3.9 days.
  • It spins fast, near 90% of the maximum, though the number depends on the model used.
