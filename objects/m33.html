Triangulum Galaxy

Spiral galaxy · 2.73 million light-years

How big is Triangulum Galaxy?

Triangulum Galaxy is about 61,100 light-years across.

See Triangulum Galaxy in 3D, at true scale

The Triangulum Galaxy is the third-largest member of the Local Group, after Andromeda and the Milky Way. It is a loose, open spiral full of bright star-forming regions.

At a glance

Diameter61,100 light-years
Mass9.94 × 10⁴⁰ kg
Diameter compared with Earth4.5 × 10¹³ × Earth
Distance2.73 million light-years
SizeAbout 61,000 light-years
StarsAbout 40 billion
Also known asM33

Good to know

  • From a very dark site it is one of the most distant things visible to the naked eye.
  • It may be a satellite of Andromeda.
  • It has no bulge at its centre and only a small black hole, if any.
