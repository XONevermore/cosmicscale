Epsilon Eridani

Orange dwarf · 10.5 light-years

How big is Epsilon Eridani?

Epsilon Eridani is about 1,023,000 km across.

See Epsilon Eridani in 3D, at true scale

Epsilon Eridani is a young orange dwarf, a little smaller and cooler than the Sun, only 10.5 light-years away. It has at least one giant planet and belts of dust and comets, like a younger version of our own Solar System.

At a glance

Diameter1,023,000 km
Mass1.63 × 10³⁰ kg
Diameter compared with Earth80.3 × Earth
Distance10.5 light-years
AgeAbout 400–800 million years
PlanetEpsilon Eridani b
TypeOrange dwarf

Good to know

  • It is the nearest star with a known planet that is visible to the naked eye.
  • It was one of the first two stars listened to for alien radio signals, in 1960.
  • It appears in many science-fiction stories, including Star Trek and Babylon 5.
