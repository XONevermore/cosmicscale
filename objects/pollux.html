Pollux

Orange giant · 34 light-years

How big is Pollux?

Pollux is about 1.3 × 10⁷ km across.

See Pollux in 3D, at true scale

Pollux is the brightest star in Gemini and the closest giant star to the Sun. It has finished burning hydrogen in its core and swollen to nine times the Sun's width — a preview of the Sun's own future.

At a glance

Diameter1.3 × 10⁷ km
Mass3.80 × 10³⁰ kg
Diameter compared with Earth989 × Earth
Distance34 light-years
Surface4,586 K
Brightness43 Suns
PlanetPollux b, confirmed 2006

Good to know

  • Its planet is more than twice Jupiter's mass and takes 590 days to orbit.
  • Despite the twin story, Pollux and Castor are not related: they lie at different distances and are different kinds of star.
  • It is one of the few giant stars close enough to study in fine detail.
