W2246-0526

Dust-hidden quasar galaxy · 12.4 billion light-years

How big is W2246-0526?

W2246-0526 is about 8,000 light-years across.

See W2246-0526 in 3D, at true scale

W2246-0526 is a galaxy whose central quasar is buried in dust, found by NASA's WISE survey. When it was reported in 2015 it was called the most luminous galaxy known; a quasar that dust does not hide, J0529-4351, has since been measured brighter. We see it as it was when the Universe was about 1.3 billion years old. The size shown is only its bright core of gas.

Good to know

  • Most of its light comes from around its central black hole, hidden behind dust.
  • It is pulling in at least three smaller neighbouring galaxies, and the ALMA telescope saw streams of dust running between them.
  • No telescope has a true-colour picture of it, so what you see here is a drawn impression.
