Virgo Supercluster

Supercluster of galaxy groups · Contains the Local Group

How big is Virgo Supercluster?

Virgo Supercluster is about 110 million light-years across.

See Virgo Supercluster in 3D, at true scale

The Virgo Supercluster is the collection of galaxy groups around the Virgo Cluster, with our Local Group sitting out near its edge. We now know it is not a standalone structure but one lobe of the far larger Laniakea.

At a glance

Diameter110 million light-years
Mass1.99 × 10⁴⁵ kg
Diameter compared with Earth8.2 × 10¹⁶ × Earth
GroupsAt least 100
CoreThe Virgo Cluster, 1,300+ galaxies
Our positionNear the outskirts

Good to know

  • The Virgo Cluster's gravity slows our local expansion, pulling the Local Group toward it at about 300 km/s.
  • M87 and its famous black hole live at the cluster's heart.
  • Superclusters like this are the largest structures that are not simply part of the cosmic web.
