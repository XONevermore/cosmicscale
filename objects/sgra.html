Sagittarius A*

Supermassive black hole · 26,700 light-years

How big is Sagittarius A*?

Sagittarius A* is about 2.5 × 10⁷ km across.

See Sagittarius A* in 3D, at true scale

Sagittarius A* is the supermassive black hole at the centre of the Milky Way, 26,700 light-years away. We knew it was there long before we saw it, because we could watch stars whipping around something invisible and enormously heavy.

At a glance

Diameter2.5 × 10⁷ km
Mass8.55 × 10³⁶ kg
Diameter compared with Earth1,990 × Earth
Distance26,700 light-years
Imaged2022, Event Horizon Telescope
Nobel Prize2020

Good to know

  • The star S301 whips around it every 8.7 years at over 8% of the speed of light, the fastest star known.
  • Tracking those stars for decades won Reinhard Genzel and Andrea Ghez the Nobel Prize in Physics.
  • It is quiet as black holes go, swallowing only a trickle of gas.
