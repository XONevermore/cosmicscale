TON 618

Ultramassive black hole · 10.4 billion light-years

How big is TON 618?

TON 618 is about 0.04 light-years across.

See TON 618 in 3D, at true scale

TON 618 is an ultramassive black hole powering a quasar so bright that we can see it from over ten billion light-years away. It was catalogued as a faint blue star in 1957 before anyone understood what quasars were.

At a glance

Diameter0.04 light-years
Mass1.31 × 10⁴¹ kg
Diameter compared with Earth3.1 × 10⁷ × Earth
Distance10.4 billion light-years
HorizonAbout 2,600 AU across
Luminosity140 trillion Suns

Good to know

  • Light takes about 15 days just to cross its event horizon.
  • The quasar outshines its entire host galaxy by a factor of thousands.
  • We see it as it was when the universe was about a quarter of its present age.
