Helix Nebula

Planetary nebula · 655 light-years

How big is Helix Nebula?

Helix Nebula is about 5.74 light-years across.

See Helix Nebula in 3D, at true scale

The Helix Nebula is the glowing shell of gas thrown off by a dying Sun-like star. The star's exposed core, now a white dwarf, lights the gas from inside. It is one of the nearest planetary nebulae to Earth.

At a glance

Diameter5.74 light-years
Diameter compared with Earth4.3 × 10⁹ × Earth
Distance655 light-years
SizeAbout 5.7 light-years across
AgeAbout 10,600 years
NicknameThe Eye of God

Good to know

  • Our Sun will make a nebula like this in about 5 billion years.
  • Its inner edge is full of thousands of comet-shaped knots of gas, each bigger than the Solar System.
  • Despite the name, planetary nebulae have nothing to do with planets.
