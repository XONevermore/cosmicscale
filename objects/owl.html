Owl Nebula

Planetary nebula (M97) · About 2,700 light-years

How big is Owl Nebula?

Owl Nebula is about 2.80 light-years across.

See Owl Nebula in 3D, at true scale

The Owl Nebula is a round planetary nebula in Ursa Major, the gas of a dying star about 2.8 light-years across. Its name comes from Lord Rosse's drawing of 1848, which showed the two dark eyes.

At a glance

DistanceAbout 2,700 light-years

AgeAbout 10,000 years
ConstellationUrsa Major

Good to know

  • The eyes are two hollow cavities in the shell of gas, lined up and tilted to our view.
  • Its gas is still expanding at about 40 km/s.
  • A faint halo around it is left from earlier puffs, over 100,000 years old.
