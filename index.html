Questions

How big is the Sun compared to Earth?

The Sun is about 109 times wider than Earth: 1,391,000 km against 12,740 km. About 1.3 million Earths would fit inside it.

How big is Jupiter compared to Earth?

Jupiter is about 11.0 times wider than Earth (139,800 km across). More than 1,300 Earths would fit inside it.

How big is the Moon compared to Earth?

Earth is about 3.67 times wider than the Moon, which is 3,475 km across.

What is the biggest star in Cosmic Scale?

Stephenson 2-18, about 3.0 × 10⁹ km across: roughly 2,150 Suns side by side. Stars this large are uncertain by tens of percent.

How big is a black hole?

The size of a black hole is the size of its event horizon, the point of no return. For Sagittarius A*, at the centre of the Milky Way, it is about 2.5 × 10⁷ km across. For the giant TON 618 it is about 0.04 light-years.
