V404 Cygni

Black hole in a triple system · About 7,800 light-years

How big is V404 Cygni?

V404 Cygni is about 53.2 km across.

See V404 Cygni in 3D, at true scale

V404 Cygni is a black hole of about 9 Suns pulling gas off a close orange star. It flared in 1938, 1989 and 2015, and was the first black hole whose distance was measured directly by parallax. This view is an illustration.

At a glance

Diameter53.2 km
Mass1.79 × 10³¹ kg
Diameter compared with Earth0.004 × Earth
Inner orbit6.5 days
Outer orbitAbout 70,000 years
DistanceAbout 7,800 light-years

Good to know

  • Because its distant partner stayed bound, the black hole must have formed with almost no kick, perhaps without a supernova.
  • Its 2015 outburst lit up dust clouds between us and it, making rings of X-ray echoes.
  • The far star is at least 3,500 times the Earth–Sun distance away.
