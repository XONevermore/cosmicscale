Milky Way

Barred spiral galaxy · You are here

How big is Milky Way?

Milky Way is about 100,000 light-years across.

See Milky Way in 3D, at true scale

Our galaxy: a barred spiral of a few hundred billion stars, with a supermassive black hole at its centre and the Sun about two-thirds of the way out. The band of light across a dark sky is its disk seen edge-on from the inside.

At a glance

Diameter100,000 light-years
Mass2.98 × 10⁴² kg
Diameter compared with Earth7.4 × 10¹³ × Earth
Stars100–400 billion
Sun's distance26,000 light-years out
Sun's orbit230 million years

Good to know

  • We have never seen it from outside; its shape is worked out from painstaking surveys taken from within.
  • It is on course to merge with Andromeda in about 4.5 billion years.
  • Its disk is warped, probably by the pull of the Magellanic Clouds.
