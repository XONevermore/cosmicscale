ESO 383-76

Supergiant elliptical galaxy · About 700 million light-years

How big is ESO 383-76?

ESO 383-76 is about 1,800,000 light-years across.

See ESO 383-76 in 3D, at true scale

ESO 383-76 is a supergiant elliptical galaxy at the heart of the galaxy cluster Abell 3571, in the Shapley Supercluster — the densest gathering of galaxies in our part of the universe. Like IC 1101 it is a cD galaxy: a huge ball of old stars wrapped in a faint, sprawling halo, grown by swallowing the galaxies around it.

Good to know

  • Its faint outer halo fades so gradually that its size depends on how deep an image goes; published figures differ widely.
  • The Shapley Supercluster around it is so massive that its gravity tugs on the Milky Way from 650 million light-years away.
  • cD galaxies like it are found only at the centres of rich clusters, where a steady supply of smaller galaxies falls in to be eaten.
