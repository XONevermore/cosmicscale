Stephenson 2-18

Red supergiant · 19,000 light-years

How big is Stephenson 2-18?

Stephenson 2-18 is about 3.0 × 10⁹ km across.

See Stephenson 2-18 in 3D, at true scale

Stephenson 2-18 is a red supergiant near the star cluster Stephenson 2, and one of the largest stars ever proposed. Whether it truly belongs to that cluster is debated — and the answer changes its distance, and so its size.

At a glance

Diameter3.0 × 10⁹ km
Diameter compared with Earth235,000 × Earth
DistanceAbout 19,000 light-years
Surface3,200 K
RadiusAround 2,150 Suns
TypeRed supergiant

Good to know

  • If the size is right, about 10 billion Suns would fit inside it.
  • Light would take nearly nine hours to travel around its circumference.
  • It lies deep in the galactic plane, so its light reaches us dimmed and reddened by dust.
