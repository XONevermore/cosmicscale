Earth

Planet · 150 million km from the Sun

How big is Earth?

Earth is about 12,740 km across.

See Earth in 3D, at true scale

The only world we know that has liquid water on its surface, an oxygen atmosphere and life. Its magnetic field shields it from the solar wind, its large Moon steadies its tilt, and its plate tectonics keep recycling the crust.

At a glance

Diameter12,740 km
Mass5.97 × 10²⁴ kg
Year365.25 days
Day23 hours 56 minutes
Surface71% water
Atmosphere78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen

Good to know

  • It is the densest planet in the Solar System.
  • Its oxygen is biological: there would be almost none in the air without living things making it.
  • Seen from Saturn, Earth is a single pale blue dot less than a pixel across.
