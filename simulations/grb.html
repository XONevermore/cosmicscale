A star's core falls in

The story begins when the core of a massive star runs out of fuel and collapses in less than a second into a black hole, ringed by a spinning disc of hot gas.

No telescope saw the star. But the supernova that followed, found by the James Webb Space Telescope months later (Blanchard et al., 2024), shows that a massive star did die. Its core, disc and jets are drawn from computer models of such collapses; nothing here has been seen.
