Meteor showers in 2026 and 2027

When the big meteor showers peak, how many meteors an hour they can bring under a dark sky, and how bright the Moon will be that night (a bright Moon hides the fainter ones).

See what is above you tonight

Cosmic Scale works these out on your phone from real orbits, tells you whether you can see each eclipse from where you are, and can remind you the evening before.

Coming up

Leonid meteor shower

17 November 2026, peak 14:52 UTC · Up to 15 meteors an hour, Moon 51% lit

Meteors are specks of dust, mostly smaller than a grain of sand, burning up high in the air as Earth crosses a trail left by a comet or an asteroid. This trail comes from 55P/Tempel–Tuttle. At best you would see 15 meteors an hour, under a perfectly dark sky with the shower overhead (its ZHR). Expect fewer. The Moon is 51% lit at the peak and will wash out the fainter meteors.
