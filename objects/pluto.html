Pluto

Dwarf planet · 5.9 billion km from the Sun

How big is Pluto?

Pluto is about 2,377 km across.

See Pluto in 3D, at true scale

Pluto was the ninth planet for 76 years, and is now the best-known dwarf planet. In 2015 New Horizons raced past and turned a fuzzy dot into a world with nitrogen glaciers, water-ice mountains and a blue haze.

At a glance

Diameter2,377 km
Mass1.30 × 10²² kg
Diameter compared with Earth0.187 × Earth
Orbit248 years
Surface−230 °C
MoonsFive
Discovered1930, Clyde Tombaugh

Good to know

  • Sputnik Planitia, the left half of its 'heart', is a glacier of nitrogen ice that churns slowly like a lava lamp.
  • For 20 years of each orbit it is closer to the Sun than Neptune.
  • Some of Tombaugh's ashes are aboard New Horizons, which is now beyond Pluto and still flying.
