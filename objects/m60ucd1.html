M60-UCD1

Ultra-compact dwarf galaxy · About 54 million light-years

How big is M60-UCD1?

M60-UCD1 is about 160 light-years across.

See M60-UCD1 in 3D, at true scale

M60-UCD1 is an ultra-compact dwarf galaxy beside the giant galaxy Messier 60. It is probably what is left of a galaxy of about 10 billion Suns whose outer stars M60 tore off. It now sits only about 21,500 light-years from M60's centre as seen on the sky.

Good to know

  • In its core, stars are about 15,000 times closer-packed than around the Sun.
  • Its black hole weighs five times the Milky Way's, in a galaxy about 500 times smaller across.
  • The black hole is about 15% of the whole galaxy's mass; in the Milky Way the share is under 0.01%.
