M32's black hole

Supermassive black hole · 2.5 million light-years

How big is M32's black hole?

M32's black hole is about 1.5 × 10⁷ km across.

See M32's black hole in 3D, at true scale

This black hole is at the centre of Messier 32, a small dense galaxy beside Andromeda. It is quiet, and this view is an illustration.

At a glance

Diameter1.5 × 10⁷ km

Good to know

  • Two different ways of weighing it, years apart, give the same mass.
  • If M32 is the stripped core of a once much bigger galaxy, such a large black hole would make sense.
  • It is so close to Andromeda in the sky that it shows up in most photos of it.
