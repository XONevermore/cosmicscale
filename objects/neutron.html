Neutron star

Collapsed stellar core · Nearest ~400 light-years

How big is Neutron star?

Neutron star is about 22.0 km across.

See Neutron star in 3D, at true scale

When a star far heavier than the Sun runs out of fuel, its core collapses in about a second and the rest of the star explodes as a supernova. What is left is a neutron star: roughly one and a half times the mass of the Sun squeezed into a ball the size of a city, held up only by the pressure of neutrons packed shoulder to shoulder.

At a glance

Fastest spin716 turns a second

Good to know

  • A sugar cube of neutron-star material would weigh about as much as Mount Everest.
  • Its surface gravity is around 200 billion times Earth's; falling one metre would take you to a tenth of the speed of light.
  • Many sweep beams of radio waves around like lighthouses. We call those pulsars, and they keep time as well as atomic clocks.
