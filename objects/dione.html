Dione

Moon of Saturn · 377,396 km from Saturn

How big is Dione?

Dione is about 1,123 km across.

See Dione in 3D, at true scale

Dione is an icy moon of Saturn with bright ice cliffs streaking its trailing side. Cassini found hints of a buried ocean beneath its crust.

At a glance

Diameter1,123 km
Mass1.09 × 10²¹ kg
Diameter compared with Earth0.088 × Earth
Size1,123 km
OrbitsSaturn, every 2.7 days
Discovered1684, Giovanni Cassini
VisitedCassini, 2004–2017

Good to know

  • The bright streaks once thought to be frost turned out to be ice cliffs hundreds of metres high.
  • It has a very thin atmosphere of oxygen ions.
  • Two small moons share its orbit, ahead of and behind it.
  • Next smaller: Quaoar · 1,110 km
  • Next larger: Charon · 1,212 km
