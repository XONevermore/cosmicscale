Andromeda's black hole

Supermassive black hole (M31*) · 2.5 million light-years

How big is Andromeda's black hole?

Andromeda's black hole is about 8.3 × 10⁸ km across.

See Andromeda's black hole in 3D, at true scale

This black hole sits at the heart of the Andromeda Galaxy, our big neighbour. It is very quiet, with no bright disk or jet, and this view is an illustration. Hubble weighed it by timing the stars that whirl around it.

GalaxyAndromeda (M31)
Distance2.5 million light-years

Good to know

  • Over 400 young blue stars race around it at about 1,000 km/s, in a disk only about a light-year across.
  • Those stars formed about 200 million years ago, right next to the black hole, which is hard to explain.
  • Andromeda's core looks double: the second 'core' is an off-centre ring of old red stars around the black hole.
