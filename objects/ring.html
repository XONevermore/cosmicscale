Ring Nebula

Planetary nebula · About 2,600 light-years

How big is Ring Nebula?

Ring Nebula is about 0.90 light-years across.

See Ring Nebula in 3D, at true scale

The Ring Nebula is the shell of gas thrown off by a dying star, now a hot white dwarf at its centre. It is really a barrel of gas that we see almost end-on, so it looks like a ring. Antoine Darquier found it in 1779.

At a glance

Diameter0.90 light-years
Diameter compared with Earth6.7 × 10⁸ × Earth
DistanceAbout 2,600 light-years
SizeAbout 1 light-year across
ConstellationLyra
Also known asM57

Good to know

  • Webb counted about 20,000 dense clumps of gas in its ring.
  • Faint arcs around it may be the trail of a small hidden companion star circling the dying star.
  • It is a barrel of gas seen end-on, not a flat ring: we look down its hollow middle.
