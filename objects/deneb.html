Deneb

White supergiant · 2,600 light-years

How big is Deneb?

Deneb is about 2.8 × 10⁸ km across.

See Deneb in 3D, at true scale

Deneb marks the tail of Cygnus the Swan and one corner of the Summer Triangle. It is far more distant than most bright stars, which means it must be extraordinarily luminous to look as bright as it does.

At a glance

Diameter2.8 × 10⁸ km
Mass3.78 × 10³¹ kg
Diameter compared with Earth22,200 × Earth
DistanceAbout 2,600 light-years
Surface8,525 K
BrightnessNearly 200,000 Suns
TypeWhite supergiant

Good to know

  • Its distance is uncertain, and with it the estimate of how luminous it really is.
  • It is shedding mass in a strong stellar wind, losing in a century what the Sun loses in a hundred thousand years.
  • It will end its life as a supernova within a few million years.
