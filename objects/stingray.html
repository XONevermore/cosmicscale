Stingray Nebula

Planetary nebula · About 5,000 light-years

How big is Stingray Nebula?

Stingray Nebula is about 0.04 light-years across.

See Stingray Nebula in 3D, at true scale

The Stingray Nebula is the youngest planetary nebula known: its gas only started to glow a few decades ago. Hubble photos from 1996 and 2016 show it dimming and changing shape fast. Its star heated up suddenly and is now cooling back down.

Good to know

  • Between 1996 and 2016 the green glow of its oxygen dropped nearly 1,000 times.
  • Its central star heated to almost ten times the Sun's surface temperature, then cooled again.
  • Astronomers have never seen a planetary nebula change this fast, within a human lifetime.
