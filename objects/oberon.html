Oberon

Moon of Uranus · 583,520 km from Uranus

How big is Oberon?

Oberon is about 1,523 km across.

See Oberon in 3D, at true scale

Oberon is the outermost of Uranus's large moons, an old, cratered world of ice and rock. Many of its craters have dark floors, perhaps where darker material welled up from below.

At a glance

Diameter compared with Earth0.120 × Earth

OrbitsUranus, every 13.5 days
Discovered1787, William Herschel
VisitedVoyager 2, 1986

Good to know

  • It has a mountain about 11 km high.
  • Its moons, like Uranus, are named after characters from Shakespeare and Pope.
  • Only about 40% of its surface has been photographed.
