NGC 1277's black hole

Black hole of a relic galaxy · About 235 million light-years

How big is NGC 1277's black hole?

NGC 1277's black hole is about 2.9 × 10¹⁰ km across.

See NGC 1277's black hole in 3D, at true scale

This black hole sits in NGC 1277, a small galaxy in the Perseus Cluster. NGC 1277 is a relic: it stopped making stars while the Universe was young, so it is like a fossil of that time. The hole is quiet, with no bright disk or jet, and this view is an illustration.

Good to know

  • The galaxy's stars all formed about 12 billion years ago.
  • It seems to have almost no dark matter: below 5% within 20,000 light-years of its centre, where about 60% was expected.
  • Fewer than 1 in 1,000 massive galaxies are relics like this one.
