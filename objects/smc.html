Small Magellanic Cloud

Dwarf irregular galaxy · 200,000 light-years

How big is Small Magellanic Cloud?

Small Magellanic Cloud is about 18,900 light-years across.

See Small Magellanic Cloud in 3D, at true scale

The Small Magellanic Cloud is a dwarf galaxy orbiting the Milky Way, a companion of the Large Magellanic Cloud. Both are being stretched by our galaxy's gravity, and a stream of gas trails behind them across the sky.

At a glance

Diameter18,900 light-years
Mass1.39 × 10⁴⁰ kg
Diameter compared with Earth1.4 × 10¹³ × Earth
Distance200,000 light-years
SizeAbout 19,000 light-years
StarsSeveral hundred million
Seen fromThe southern sky

Good to know

  • Studying its stars, Henrietta Leavitt found the law that let astronomers measure distances to other galaxies.
  • It is visible to the naked eye as a faint cloud, like a piece of the Milky Way broken off.
  • It and the Large Cloud were named after Ferdinand Magellan, whose crew recorded them in 1519.
