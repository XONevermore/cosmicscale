Pleiades

Open star cluster · 444 light-years

How big is Pleiades?

Pleiades is about 86.0 light-years across.

See Pleiades in 3D, at true scale

The Pleiades, or Seven Sisters, is a cluster of young, hot blue stars — among the nearest to Earth and one of the most obvious to the naked eye. A faint blue haze around them is dust that the cluster is passing through.

At a glance

Japanese nameSubaru

Good to know

  • Subaru's six-star logo is the Pleiades.
  • Its blue glow is not left over from the stars' birth; they are just passing through an unrelated cloud.
  • It appears in the myths of cultures all over the world.
