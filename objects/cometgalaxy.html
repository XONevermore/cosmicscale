Comet Galaxy

Spiral being torn apart by a cluster · About 2.8 billion light-years

How big is Comet Galaxy?

Comet Galaxy is about 330,000 light-years across.

See Comet Galaxy in 3D, at true scale

The Comet Galaxy is a spiral falling through the galaxy cluster Abell 2667. The cluster's gravity and hot gas are pulling it apart, leaving a trail of bright blue knots behind it. As seen on the sky the trail reaches about 260,000 light-years; models put its true length at 290,000 to 460,000.

Good to know

  • Millions of its stars have been pulled out and left behind along the trail.
  • Being torn apart has set off a burst of star birth: about 53 Suns' worth of new stars every year.
  • It should end up as a galaxy with almost no gas; the whole change takes close to a billion years.
