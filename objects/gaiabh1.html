Gaia BH1

Dormant black hole · 1,560 light-years

How big is Gaia BH1?

Gaia BH1 is about 56.8 km across.

See Gaia BH1 in 3D, at true scale

Gaia BH1 is the closest known black hole to Earth, found not by any glow but by watching a Sun-like star swing around something invisible. It is dormant: no disk, no X-rays, nothing falling in.

At a glance

Diameter56.8 km
Mass1.91 × 10³¹ kg
Diameter compared with Earth0.004 × Earth
Distance1,560 light-years
Orbit186 days
Found2022, from Gaia data

Good to know

  • Its companion orbits at roughly Earth's distance from the Sun — a normal star circling a black hole.
  • How such a pair survived the supernova that made the black hole is still unexplained.
  • Most known black holes are eating; quiet ones like this are likely far more common and far harder to find.
