Exoplanets

HAT-P-67b

HAT-P-67b is one of the largest planets known and one of the least dense — a Saturn-mass world blown up to more than twice Jupiter's width, trailing a vast cloud of escaping helium.

TrES-4b

TrES-4b is a hot Jupiter so puffed up by its star's heat that its density is lower than cork.

55 Cancri e

55 Cancri e is a super-Earth so close to its star that its year lasts 18 hours and its day side may be an ocean of magma.

Kepler-452b

Kepler-452b was announced in 2015 as the closest thing yet to an Earth twin: a slightly larger world orbiting a Sun-like star on a 385-day year, right in the habitable zone.

TRAPPIST-1e

TRAPPIST-1e is one of seven Earth-sized planets orbiting a small red dwarf 40 light-years away.
