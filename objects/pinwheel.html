Pinwheel Galaxy

Face-on spiral galaxy (M101) · 22 million light-years

How big is Pinwheel Galaxy?

Pinwheel Galaxy is about 170,000 light-years across.

See Pinwheel Galaxy in 3D, at true scale

The Pinwheel Galaxy, M101, is a large spiral that we see face-on. Its bright disk is about 170,000 light-years across, and faint stars reach much further. It is lopsided: the pull of its companion galaxies has stretched its arms further on one side.

Good to know

  • Supernova SN 2011fe went off here and was caught only about 11 hours after it exploded.
  • In 2023 Koichi Itagaki, an amateur astronomer in Japan, found another one here: SN 2023ixf.
  • Hubble found nearly 3,000 star clusters here that had never been seen before.
