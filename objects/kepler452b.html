Kepler-452b

Super-Earth · 1,800 light-years

How big is Kepler-452b?

Kepler-452b is about 20,770 km across.

See Kepler-452b in 3D, at true scale

Kepler-452b was announced in 2015 as the closest thing yet to an Earth twin: a slightly larger world orbiting a Sun-like star on a 385-day year, right in the habitable zone. Later analysis has questioned whether the signal is a planet at all.

At a glance

Diameter compared with Earth1.63 × Earth
Distance1,800 light-years
Year385 days
Size1.6× Earth
StarSun-like, 6 billion years old

Good to know

  • Its star is older and brighter than the Sun, so the planet may be entering a runaway greenhouse phase.
  • At that distance, confirming it will need instruments we do not yet have.
  • It was nicknamed 'Earth's older cousin' rather than a twin, for good reason.
