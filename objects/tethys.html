Tethys

Moon of Saturn · 294,619 km from Saturn

How big is Tethys?

Tethys is about 1,062 km across.

See Tethys in 3D, at true scale

Tethys is a mid-sized moon of Saturn made almost entirely of water ice. A huge crater covers much of one side, and a canyon system runs three-quarters of the way around it.

At a glance

Diameter1,062 km
Mass6.17 × 10²⁰ kg
Diameter compared with Earth0.083 × Earth
Size1,062 km
OrbitsSaturn, every 1.9 days
Big craterOdysseus, 450 km wide
CanyonIthaca Chasma, 2,000 km long

Good to know

  • It is one of the most reflective bodies in the Solar System.
  • Its density is close to that of water, so it is almost pure ice.
  • Two tiny moons, Telesto and Calypso, share its orbit.
