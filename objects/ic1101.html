IC 1101

Supergiant elliptical galaxy · 1 billion light-years

How big is IC 1101?

IC 1101 is about 4,000,000 light-years across.

See IC 1101 in 3D, at true scale

IC 1101 sits at the centre of a rich galaxy cluster and is one of the largest galaxies known. It is a supergiant elliptical: no spiral arms, little new star formation, just a vast swarm of ageing stars built up by swallowing smaller galaxies.

At a glance

Discovered1790, William Herschel

Good to know

  • Its size is quoted anywhere from 600,000 to 4 million light-years, depending on where you decide its faint halo ends.
  • Most of its stars are old and red; it has largely stopped making new ones.
  • It grew by cannibalising other galaxies in its cluster over billions of years.
