Antares

Red supergiant · 550 light-years

How big is Antares?

Antares is about 9.5 × 10⁸ km across.

See Antares in 3D, at true scale

Antares is the red heart of Scorpius, a supergiant so large that if it replaced the Sun it would swallow the orbit of Mars. Its name means 'rival of Mars', for its similar colour.

At a glance

Diameter9.5 × 10⁸ km
Mass2.69 × 10³¹ kg
Diameter compared with Earth74,300 × Earth
Distance550 light-years
Surface3,660 K
Brightness75,000 Suns
CompanionA hot blue star, Antares B

Good to know

  • It is losing mass so fast that it is surrounded by a glowing nebula of its own gas.
  • Its outer layers are so thin that in places the 'surface' is less dense than a laboratory vacuum.
  • It will explode as a supernova, probably within the next million years.
