H1821+643

Quasar in a galaxy cluster · 3.4 billion light-years

How big is H1821+643?

H1821+643 is about 2.4 × 10¹⁰ km across.

See H1821+643 in 3D, at true scale

H1821+643 is a quasar: a black hole swallowing gas so fast that it outshines its galaxy. Its mass is disputed. Most methods give a few billion Suns, one gives 30 billion, so the size shown is for 4 billion. This view is an illustration.

Good to know

  • It is the closest quasar that sits in the middle of a galaxy cluster.
  • Despite its power, it does little to stop the cluster's gas from cooling.
  • Its mass estimates run from about 1 to 30 billion Suns, depending on the method.
