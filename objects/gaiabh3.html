Gaia BH3

Dormant black hole · 1,926 light-years

How big is Gaia BH3?

Gaia BH3 is about 195 km across.

See Gaia BH3 in 3D, at true scale

Gaia BH3 is a black hole of 33 Suns found in 2024 from the wobble of a star orbiting it every 11.6 years. It is the most massive stellar black hole known in our galaxy, and one of the nearest.

At a glance

Diameter195 km
Mass6.56 × 10³¹ kg
Diameter compared with Earth0.015 × Earth
Distance1,926 light-years
Found2024, from Gaia data
CompanionAn old, metal-poor giant star

Good to know

  • Its mass is like the black holes whose collisions are picked up as gravitational waves.
  • Its companion is almost pure hydrogen and helium, a sign it came from an early generation of stars.
  • It gives off no light; it was found only by its pull on the other star.
