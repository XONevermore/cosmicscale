TrES-4b

Puffy hot Jupiter · 1,570 light-years

How big is TrES-4b?

TrES-4b is about 250,300 km across.

See TrES-4b in 3D, at true scale

TrES-4b is a hot Jupiter so puffed up by its star's heat that its density is lower than cork. It orbits in three and a half days and is probably losing its atmosphere into space.

At a glance

Mass9.30 × 10²⁶ kg

Good to know

  • It is nearly twice Jupiter's width but only half its mass.
  • Its gravity is so weak that its upper atmosphere may be streaming away in a comet-like tail.
  • Planets like this are hard to explain: heating alone does not seem to account for how inflated they are.
