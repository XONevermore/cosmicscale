Dwarf planets

Pluto

Pluto was the ninth planet for 76 years, and is now the best-known dwarf planet.

Eris

Eris is slightly smaller than Pluto but noticeably heavier.

Haumea

Haumea is a dwarf planet beyond Neptune that spins so fast — once every 3.9 hours — that it has been stretched into an egg shape about twice as long as it is wide.

Makemake

Makemake is a bright, reddish world of methane ice in the Kuiper Belt, discovered shortly after Easter 2005 and nicknamed 'Easterbunny' before it was named after the creator god of Rapa Nui.

Gonggong

Gonggong is one of the largest dwarf planets, a reddish world of ice on a long, tilted orbit far beyond Neptune.

Quaoar

Quaoar is a dwarf planet in the Kuiper belt, about half the size of Pluto.

Sedna

Sedna is one of the most distant known worlds in the Solar System.

Ceres

Ceres is the largest object in the asteroid belt and the only dwarf planet inside Neptune's orbit.
