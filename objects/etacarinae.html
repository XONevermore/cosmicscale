Eta Carinae

Luminous blue variable · 7,500 light-years

How big is Eta Carinae?

Eta Carinae is about 3.3 × 10⁸ km across.

See Eta Carinae in 3D, at true scale

Eta Carinae is one of the most massive and luminous stars in our galaxy, about 5 million times brighter than the Sun. In 1843 it erupted and briefly became the second-brightest star in the sky, throwing off the Homunculus Nebula. Its size is uncertain: estimates range from 60 to over 800 Suns across, and the value shown here is a middle one.

Good to know

  • It is really two stars; the smaller one orbits every 5.5 years.
  • It is expected to explode as a supernova, perhaps within the next hundred thousand years.
  • It lives inside the Carina Nebula.
