Sombrero Galaxy

Spiral galaxy, seen almost edge-on · 31 million light-years

How big is Sombrero Galaxy?

Sombrero Galaxy is about 49,000 light-years across.

See Sombrero Galaxy in 3D, at true scale

The Sombrero Galaxy is a spiral seen almost edge-on, with a huge glowing bulge crossed by a dark lane of dust — like a wide-brimmed hat. At its centre is a black hole of about a billion Suns.

Good to know

  • It has around 2,000 globular star clusters, ten times as many as the Milky Way.
  • Webb's infrared view shows the dust ring clumpy and the bulge nearly invisible.
  • It is bright enough to see in a small telescope in Virgo.
