HR 5171 A

Yellow hypergiant · 12,000 light-years

How big is HR 5171 A?

HR 5171 A is about 1.8 × 10⁹ km across.

See HR 5171 A in 3D, at true scale

HR 5171 A is a rare yellow hypergiant, and rarer still for having a companion star so close that the two touch, sharing an envelope of gas. It is among the largest yellow stars known.

At a glance

Diameter1.8 × 10⁹ km
Mass7.76 × 10³¹ kg
Diameter compared with Earth144,000 × Earth
DistanceAbout 12,000 light-years
RadiusAbout 1,300 Suns
BrightnessAround 1 million Suns
TypeYellow hypergiant, contact binary

Good to know

  • Its companion orbits inside the hypergiant's own atmosphere, taking about 1,300 days.
  • Yellow hypergiants are a brief, unstable stage: only a dozen or so are known in our galaxy.
  • It has been slowly cooling and swelling over the last forty years of observation.
