Neptune

Ice giant · 4.5 billion km from the Sun

How big is Neptune?

Neptune is about 49,240 km across.

See Neptune in 3D, at true scale

Neptune was found with mathematics before anyone looked: astronomers noticed Uranus was being tugged off course, calculated where the culprit must be, and pointed a telescope there in 1846. It is an ice giant with the fiercest winds in the Solar System.

At a glance

Moons16 known

Good to know

  • It has completed only one orbit since its discovery, in 2011.
  • Its blue colour comes from methane absorbing red light — though its true shade is paler than the famous Voyager images suggest.
  • Voyager 2, in 1989, is the only spacecraft ever to visit.
