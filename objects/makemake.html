Makemake

Dwarf planet · 6.8 billion km from the Sun

How big is Makemake?

Makemake is about 1,430 km across.

See Makemake in 3D, at true scale

Makemake is a bright, reddish world of methane ice in the Kuiper Belt, discovered shortly after Easter 2005 and nicknamed 'Easterbunny' before it was named after the creator god of Rapa Nui.

At a glance

Diameter1,430 km
Mass3.10 × 10²¹ kg
Diameter compared with Earth0.112 × Earth
Orbit305 years
SurfaceMethane and ethane ice, −240 °C
MoonsOne, found in 2016
Discovered2005

Good to know

  • Its discovery, along with Eris, forced the debate that redefined what counts as a planet.
  • Its moon MK2 is very dark, which is why it went unnoticed for eleven years.
  • It has no thick atmosphere, unlike Pluto — a surprise when astronomers watched it pass in front of a star.
