Mimas

Moon of Saturn · 1.4 billion km from the Sun

How big is Mimas?

Mimas is about 396 km across.

See Mimas in 3D, at true scale

Mimas is the smallest body in the Solar System still rounded by its own gravity. One enormous impact crater, Herschel, spans a third of its width and gives it an uncanny resemblance to a certain fictional battle station.

At a glance

Diameter396 km
Mass3.75 × 10¹⁹ kg
Diameter compared with Earth0.031 × Earth
OrbitsSaturn, every 22.6 hours
Herschel crater130 km across
SurfaceAbout −200 °C
Discovered1789, William Herschel

Good to know

  • The impact that made Herschel nearly shattered the moon; fractures show on the opposite side.
  • Measurements published in 2024 suggest a young liquid ocean may lie 20–30 km beneath the ice.
  • Mimas is mostly water ice, which is why so small a body is round at all.
