Cygnus X-1

Stellar-mass black hole · 7,200 light-years

How big is Cygnus X-1?

Cygnus X-1 is about 125 km across.

See Cygnus X-1 in 3D, at true scale

Cygnus X-1 is a black hole of about 21 Suns locked in a 5-day orbit with a blue supergiant star. Gas streaming off the companion spirals in, heats to millions of degrees and glows in X-rays, which is how it was found in 1964 by a detector carried above the atmosphere on a sounding rocket.

Good to know

  • Stephen Hawking bet Kip Thorne that it was not a black hole, as insurance against his own life's work being wrong. He conceded the bet in 1990.
  • Its event horizon is only about 125 km across, but you would need to look from within a few thousand kilometres to see it bend light.
  • The gas reaches roughly 10 million degrees before it crosses the horizon.
