Ceres

Dwarf planet · Asteroid belt

How big is Ceres?

Ceres is about 939 km across.

See Ceres in 3D, at true scale

Ceres is the largest object in the asteroid belt and the only dwarf planet inside Neptune's orbit. When it was discovered in 1801 it was called a planet; as more asteroids turned up it was reclassified, and in 2006 it was promoted to dwarf planet.

At a glance

Diameter939 km
Mass9.38 × 10²⁰ kg
Diameter compared with Earth0.074 × Earth
Orbit4.6 years, in the asteroid belt
Share of the beltAbout a third of its mass
WaterIce and brine beneath the crust
VisitedDawn, 2015–2018

Good to know

  • The bright patches in Occator crater are salt left behind by briny water that reached the surface.
  • Dawn found organic molecules, and evidence of a muddy layer of brine still present today.
  • It holds more fresh water than Earth, mostly as ice.
