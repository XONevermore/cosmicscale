Messier 4

Globular star cluster · 6,000 light-years

How big is Messier 4?

Messier 4 is about 60.0 light-years across.

See Messier 4 in 3D, at true scale

Messier 4 is a globular star cluster near Antares in Scorpius, and the closest one to Earth. It is loose for its kind, with a bar of brighter stars across its middle. Its stars are up to 13 billion years old.

At a glance

ConstellationScorpius

Good to know

  • It may hide a black hole of about 800 Suns at its centre, or a swarm of smaller dead stars: nobody can yet tell which.
  • Its faintest white dwarfs, the cooling cores of dead stars, are used as a clock to date the cluster.
  • It was born about ten times heavier and has been losing stars to the Milky Way ever since.
