Biggest nebulae and star clusters compared

Nebulae are clouds of gas and dust where stars are born or have died. They are measured in light-years: the distance light travels in a year, about 9.5 trillion km.

Nebulae & clusters

Tarantula Nebula

The Tarantula Nebula, in the Large Magellanic Cloud, is the largest and most active stellar nursery near us.

Carina Nebula

The Carina Nebula is one of the largest and brightest star-forming clouds in the Milky Way, four times as large as the Orion Nebula.

Omega Centauri

Omega Centauri is the largest globular cluster orbiting the Milky Way: around ten million ancient stars in a ball 150 light-years across.

Pleiades

The Pleiades, or Seven Sisters, is a cluster of young, hot blue stars — among the nearest to Earth and one of the most obvious to the naked eye.

Eagle Nebula

The Eagle Nebula is a cloud of gas and dust where new stars are forming, lit by a young cluster at its centre.

Orion Nebula

The Orion Nebula is the nearest large stellar nursery, a glowing cavern of gas being carved out by the young stars inside it.

Crab Nebula

The Crab Nebula is the wreckage of a star that exploded in AD 1054, bright enough to be seen in daylight for three weeks and recorded by Chinese and Japanese astronomers.

Helix Nebula

The Helix Nebula is the glowing shell of gas thrown off by a dying Sun-like star.

Eskimo Nebula

The Eskimo Nebula is the glowing gas from a dying Sun-like star, seen face-on.

Twin Jet Nebula

The Twin Jet Nebula is a cloud thrown off by a dying star with a white dwarf partner.

Oyster Nebula

The Oyster Nebula is a shell of gas blown off by a dying star.

Hourglass Nebula

The Hourglass Nebula is gas thrown off by a dying star, pinched at the waist into an hourglass.

Ring Nebula

The Ring Nebula is the shell of gas thrown off by a dying star, now a hot white dwarf at its centre.

NGC 7662

NGC 7662 is a shell of gas around a dying star in Andromeda.

IC 4593

IC 4593 is a cloud of gas puffed off by a dying star in Hercules.

Cat's Eye Nebula

The Cat's Eye Nebula is the cast-off gas of a dying star, and one of the most detailed planetary nebulae known.

Little Ghost Nebula

The Little Ghost Nebula is a ring of gas thrown off by a dying star, seen close to the centre of our galaxy in the sky.

Saturn Nebula

The Saturn Nebula is the glowing shell of gas thrown off by a dying Sun-like star.

IC 418

IC 418 is the outer layers of a red giant star, thrown off only a few thousand years ago.

NGC 3918

NGC 3918 is the glowing gas around a dying star in Centaurus.

Egg Nebula

The Egg Nebula is not yet a planetary nebula but a stage just before one.

Stingray Nebula

The Stingray Nebula is the youngest planetary nebula known: its gas only started to glow a few decades ago.
