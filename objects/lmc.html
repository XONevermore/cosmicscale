Large Magellanic Cloud

Irregular satellite galaxy · 160,000 light-years

How big is Large Magellanic Cloud?

Large Magellanic Cloud is about 32,200 light-years across.

See Large Magellanic Cloud in 3D, at true scale

The Large Magellanic Cloud is the Milky Way's biggest companion galaxy, an irregular spiral distorted by our gravity. It is a favourite laboratory: close enough to resolve individual stars, far enough to see the whole thing at once.

At a glance

Diameter32,200 light-years
Mass2.78 × 10⁴¹ kg
Diameter compared with Earth2.4 × 10¹³ × Earth
Distance160,000 light-years
StarsAbout 20 billion
ContainsThe Tarantula Nebula

Good to know

  • SN 1987A, the closest supernova in four centuries, went off here and is still being studied.
  • It may be on its first pass by the Milky Way rather than in a long-term orbit.
  • From the southern hemisphere it looks like a detached piece of the Milky Way.
