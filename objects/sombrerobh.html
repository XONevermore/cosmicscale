Sombrero's black hole

Supermassive black hole · 31 million light-years

How big is Sombrero's black hole?

Sombrero's black hole is about 3.9 × 10⁹ km across.

See Sombrero's black hole in 3D, at true scale

This black hole is at the centre of the Sombrero Galaxy, inside its huge glowing bulge. It feeds only weakly, and this view is an illustration.

At a glance

Diameter3.9 × 10⁹ km
Mass1.31 × 10³⁹ kg
Diameter compared with Earth306,000 × Earth
Range540 to 900 million Suns
GalaxySombrero (M104)
Distance31 million light-years

Good to know

  • It weighs over 150 times as much as Sagittarius A*, our galaxy's black hole.
  • It was one of the first black holes found from the motions of stars, in the late 1980s.
  • Different ways of weighing it agree to within a factor of two.
