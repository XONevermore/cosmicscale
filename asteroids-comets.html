Asteroids and comets range from boulders to worlds hundreds of kilometres wide. These are some of the best studied, visited by spacecraft or seen for centuries.

Asteroids & comets

Vesta

Vesta is the second-largest body in the asteroid belt and the brightest asteroid in our sky.

Halley's Comet

Halley's Comet is the most famous comet of all: the first known to return on a schedule, and the only bright one that does so within a human lifetime.

Comet 67P

67P/Churyumov–Gerasimenko is a comet shaped like a rubber duck, two lumps joined by a neck.

Bennu

Bennu is a rubble-pile asteroid about half a kilometre wide: loose rocks and boulders held together by their own feeble gravity.
