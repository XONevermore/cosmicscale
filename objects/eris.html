Eris

Dwarf planet · 10 billion km from the Sun

How big is Eris?

Eris is about 2,326 km across.

See Eris in 3D, at true scale

Eris is slightly smaller than Pluto but noticeably heavier. Finding it in 2005 made the question unavoidable: either the Solar System had a tenth planet, or Pluto was something else. Astronomers chose the second answer, and named the newcomer after the goddess of discord.

At a glance

Diameter2,326 km
Mass1.66 × 10²² kg
Diameter compared with Earth0.183 × Earth
Orbit558 years
Distance nowAbout 95 AU from the Sun
MoonDysnomia

Good to know

  • Its surface is coated in nitrogen and methane frost that may be its atmosphere, frozen out as it moves away from the Sun.
  • It reflects 96% of the light that hits it — almost as bright as fresh snow.
  • At its most distant it is three times farther from the Sun than Pluto.
