Bennu

Near-Earth asteroid · Orbits the Sun near Earth

How big is Bennu?

Bennu is about 490 m across.

See Bennu in 3D, at true scale

Bennu is a rubble-pile asteroid about half a kilometre wide: loose rocks and boulders held together by their own feeble gravity. NASA's OSIRIS-REx mapped it for two years, grabbed a sample from its surface in 2020 and returned it to Earth in 2023.

Good to know

  • When the spacecraft touched it, the surface gave way like a ball pit; it would have sunk in had it not fired its thrusters.
  • Its sample contains the building blocks of life, including all five bases found in DNA and RNA.
  • It has a small chance of hitting Earth in the late 2100s, about 1 in 2,700.
