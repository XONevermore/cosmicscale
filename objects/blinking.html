Blinking Nebula

Planetary nebula (NGC 6826) · About 5,000 light-years

How big is Blinking Nebula?

Blinking Nebula is about 0.80 light-years across.

See Blinking Nebula in 3D, at true scale

The Blinking Nebula, NGC 6826, is a planetary nebula with several shells. William Herschel found it in 1793. Older sources put it at about 2,000 light-years; the Gaia spacecraft's measurement of about 5,000 is the one used here.

Good to know

  • The blink happens in your eye: the bright star overwhelms the faint nebula when you stare straight at it.
  • Two bright red knots, called FLIERs, sit on either side: clumps of gas shooting outward that nobody fully understands.
  • Its central star is one of the brightest in any planetary nebula.
