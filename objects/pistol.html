Pistol Star

Blue hypergiant · 26,000 light-years

How big is Pistol Star?

Pistol Star is about 4.3 × 10⁸ km across.

See Pistol Star in 3D, at true scale

The Pistol Star is a luminous blue variable near the centre of the galaxy, hidden behind so much dust that it was only found in the 1990s with infrared eyes. It has already blown off a nebula several light-years across.

At a glance

Diameter4.3 × 10⁸ km
Mass5.47 × 10³¹ kg
Diameter compared with Earth33,400 × Earth
Distance26,000 light-years
Brightness1.6 million Suns
NebulaAbout 4 light-years across
Found1990s, by Hubble

Good to know

  • The nebula around it contains as much material as several Suns, all thrown off in a few thousand years.
  • It belongs to the Quintuplet Cluster, one of the densest star clusters in the galaxy.
  • Visible light from it never reaches us; only infrared gets through the dust.
