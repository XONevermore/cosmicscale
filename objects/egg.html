Egg Nebula

Pre-planetary nebula · About 1,000 light-years

How big is Egg Nebula?

Egg Nebula is about 0.14 light-years across.

See Egg Nebula in 3D, at true scale

The Egg Nebula is not yet a planetary nebula but a stage just before one. Its star is hidden inside a thick cocoon of dust, and its light escapes through two holes to light up beams and shells of dust. In a few thousand years the star will be hot enough to make the gas glow.

Good to know

  • The gas does not glow yet: it shines only because dust reflects the hidden star's light.
  • The pre-planetary stage lasts only a few thousand years, a blink of time for a star.
  • Its ring-like arcs are shells the star puffed out one after another, long before the beams.
