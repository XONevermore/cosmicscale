S301

Star orbiting Sagittarius A* · 26,700 light-years

How big is S301?

S301 is about 2,087,000 km across.

See S301 in 3D, at true scale

S301 is a star a little bigger than the Sun, discovered in 2026 on the tightest known orbit around Sagittarius A*. Every 8.7 years it plunges to 12 times the Earth–Sun distance from the black hole, fast and close enough that it may reveal how quickly the black hole spins.

Good to know

  • At its top speed of 25,600 km/s, over 8% of the speed of light, it would cross from the Earth to the Moon in 15 seconds.
  • It was found with the GRAVITY+ instrument on ESO's Very Large Telescope; it next swings past the black hole in 2031.
  • It was probably one of a pair: the black hole captured it and flung its partner away.
