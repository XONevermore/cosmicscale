Proxima Centauri

Red dwarf · 4.25 light-years

How big is Proxima Centauri?

Proxima Centauri is about 214,600 km across.

See Proxima Centauri in 3D, at true scale

Proxima Centauri is the closest star to the Sun, a small red dwarf gravitationally tied to the bright pair of Alpha Centauri. It is too faint to see with the naked eye despite being our nearest neighbour.

At a glance

Diameter214,600 km
Mass2.43 × 10²⁹ kg
Diameter compared with Earth16.8 × Earth
Distance4.25 light-years
Surface3,042 K
PlanetsAt least two

Good to know

  • Proxima b orbits in the zone where liquid water is possible — the nearest such planet to Earth that we know of.
  • It is a flare star: it can brighten enormously in minutes, which may be bad news for life on its planets.
  • Travelling at the speed of Voyager 1, the trip would take about 73,000 years.
