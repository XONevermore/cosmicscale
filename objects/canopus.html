Canopus

Bright giant · 310 light-years

How big is Canopus?

Canopus is about 9.9 × 10⁷ km across.

See Canopus in 3D, at true scale

Canopus is the second-brightest star in the night sky and a favourite of spacecraft: its brightness and position make it an excellent reference point for navigation far from Earth.

At a glance

Diameter9.9 × 10⁷ km
Mass1.59 × 10³¹ kg
Diameter compared with Earth7,750 × Earth
Distance310 light-years
Surface7,400 K
Brightness10,700 Suns
TypeBright giant

Good to know

  • Many spacecraft have carried a 'Canopus star tracker' to work out which way they are pointing.
  • It is too far south to be seen from most of Europe and the northern United States.
  • It is burning helium in its core, a late and fairly brief stage of a big star's life.
