Haumea

Dwarf planet · About 50 AU from the Sun

How big is Haumea?

Haumea is about 1,560 km across.

See Haumea in 3D, at true scale

Haumea is a dwarf planet beyond Neptune that spins so fast — once every 3.9 hours — that it has been stretched into an egg shape about twice as long as it is wide. It has two moons and a ring.

At a glance

Diameter1,560 km
Mass4.01 × 10²¹ kg
Diameter compared with Earth0.122 × Earth
ShapeAbout 2,100 × 1,700 × 1,000 km
Day3.9 hours
Orbit284 years
MoonsHiʻiaka and Namaka

Good to know

  • It is the fastest-spinning large object in the Solar System.
  • Its ring, found in 2017, was the first seen around a body beyond Neptune.
  • It is named after the Hawaiian goddess of childbirth; its moons after her daughters.
