Jupiter

Gas giant · 778 million km from the Sun

How big is Jupiter?

Jupiter is about 139,800 km across.

See Jupiter in 3D, at true scale

Jupiter has two and a half times the mass of every other planet put together. It is a ball of hydrogen and helium with no solid surface, banded by jet streams and dotted with storms that last for centuries.

At a glance

Diameter139,800 km
Mass1.90 × 10²⁷ kg
Diameter compared with Earth11.0 × Earth
Year11.9 years
Day9 hours 56 minutes — the fastest
Moons97 known

Good to know

  • The Great Red Spot is a storm wider than Earth that has been watched for at least 190 years, and is slowly shrinking.
  • Its magnetic field is the strongest of any planet, and its auroras are hundreds of times more powerful than Earth's.
  • Deep inside, the pressure may squeeze hydrogen into a liquid metal — the source of that magnetic field.
