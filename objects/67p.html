Comet 67P

Churyumov–Gerasimenko · Orbits the Sun every 6.4 years

How big is Comet 67P?

Comet 67P is about 4.0 km across.

See Comet 67P in 3D, at true scale

67P/Churyumov–Gerasimenko is a comet shaped like a rubber duck, two lumps joined by a neck. ESA's Rosetta orbited it for two years from 2014 and dropped the Philae lander onto its surface — the first landing on a comet.

At a glance

Diameter compared with Earth0.000 × Earth

VisitedRosetta and Philae, 2014–2016
DensityAbout half that of water

Good to know

  • Philae bounced twice and came to rest in a shadow, but still sent back data.
  • Its water has a different mix of hydrogen types from Earth's oceans, so comets like it did not bring most of our water.
  • Rosetta ended its mission by touching down on the comet in 2016.
