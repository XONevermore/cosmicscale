WOH G64

Hypergiant · 160,000 light-years

How big is WOH G64?

WOH G64 is about 2.1 × 10⁹ km across.

See WOH G64 in 3D, at true scale

WOH G64 is a hypergiant in the Large Magellanic Cloud, wrapped in a thick cocoon of dust it has thrown off. In 2024 it became the first star outside our galaxy to be imaged close up.

At a glance

Diameter2.1 × 10⁹ km
Mass4.97 × 10³¹ kg
Diameter compared with Earth168,000 × Earth
Distance160,000 light-years
RadiusAround 1,500 Suns
GalaxyLarge Magellanic Cloud
Imaged2024, VLT Interferometer

Good to know

  • The image revealed an egg-shaped cocoon of dust around it, a sign it is shedding mass fast.
  • It has dimmed markedly over the last decade, suggesting rapid change.
  • It is a strong candidate to explode as a supernova in astronomical terms — soon.
