Mars

Planet · 228 million km from the Sun

How big is Mars?

Mars is about 6,779 km across.

See Mars in 3D, at true scale

Mars is a cold desert with seasons, polar caps and the tallest volcano in the Solar System. Rivers ran on its surface billions of years ago, and finding out whether anything lived in them is the reason so many spacecraft go there.

At a glance

Diameter6,779 km
Mass6.42 × 10²³ kg
Diameter compared with Earth0.532 × Earth
Year687 days
Day24 hours 37 minutes
Temperature−140 to 20 °C
MoonsPhobos and Deimos

Good to know

  • Olympus Mons rises 22 km, nearly three times the height of Everest, and is as wide as France.
  • Valles Marineris runs 4,000 km along the equator — it would stretch across the United States.
  • Its rusty colour is iron oxide dust, the same chemistry as rust on Earth.
  • Next smaller: Ganymede · 5,268 km
  • Next larger: Sirius B · 11,690 km
