NGC 2346

Butterfly planetary nebula · About 4,700 light-years

How big is NGC 2346?

NGC 2346 is about 2.80 light-years across.

See NGC 2346 in 3D, at true scale

NGC 2346 is a bipolar planetary nebula: two big bubbles blown from a dense ring at its waist, like a butterfly. Older sources put it at about 2,000 light-years; the Gaia spacecraft's measurement of about 4,700 is the one used here.

Good to know

  • One star likely swelled up and swallowed the other, which spiralled in and threw gas out into a ring.
  • A later fast wind then blew two bubbles out of that ring, making the butterfly.
  • The companion, at about 3.5 Suns or more, is the heaviest known in such a swallowed pair, though the figure is uncertain.
