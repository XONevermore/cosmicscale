Deimos

Moon of Mars · 23,460 km from Mars

How big is Deimos?

Deimos is about 12.4 km across.

See Deimos in 3D, at true scale

Deimos is the smaller and outer of the two moons of Mars, a lumpy, dusty body barely 12 km across. It is probably a captured asteroid or debris from an ancient impact on Mars.

At a glance

Diameter12.4 km
Mass1.48 × 10¹⁵ kg
Diameter compared with Earth0.001 × Earth
SizeAbout 15 × 12 × 11 km
OrbitsMars, every 30.3 hours
Discovered1877, Asaph Hall
NameGreek for dread

Good to know

  • Its gravity is so weak that a good jump could launch you into space.
  • Seen from Mars it looks like a bright star, not a moon.
  • Its surface is smoothed by a thick layer of fine dust.
