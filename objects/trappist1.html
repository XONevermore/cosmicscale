TRAPPIST-1

Red dwarf · 40 light-years

How big is TRAPPIST-1?

TRAPPIST-1 is about 165,900 km across.

See TRAPPIST-1 in 3D, at true scale

TRAPPIST-1 is a cool red dwarf barely larger than Jupiter, and it has seven Earth-sized planets — the largest family of rocky worlds known around one star. Three of them orbit where liquid water could be possible.

Good to know

  • All seven orbits would fit comfortably inside Mercury's; the innermost planet's year lasts 1.5 days.
  • From one planet's sky the others would appear larger than our Moon.
  • Red dwarfs burn so slowly that this star will still be shining in trillions of years.
