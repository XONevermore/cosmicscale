Rhea

Moon of Saturn · 527,108 km from Saturn

How big is Rhea?

Rhea is about 1,528 km across.

See Rhea in 3D, at true scale

Rhea is Saturn's second-largest moon, a heavily cratered ball of ice with a little rock. It is cold, old and quiet, its surface little changed for billions of years.

At a glance

Diameter1,528 km
Mass2.31 × 10²¹ kg
Diameter compared with Earth0.120 × Earth
Size1,527 km
OrbitsSaturn, every 4.5 days
Discovered1672, Giovanni Cassini
VisitedCassini, 2004–2017

Good to know

  • Cassini found a thin atmosphere of oxygen and carbon dioxide around it.
  • It may once have had a faint ring, but later searches did not confirm it.
  • It is the ninth-largest moon in the Solar System.
