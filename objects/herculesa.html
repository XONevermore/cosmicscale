Hercules A

Radio galaxy · 2.1 billion light-years

How big is Hercules A?

Hercules A is about 920,000 light-years across.

See Hercules A in 3D, at true scale

Hercules A is a giant elliptical galaxy whose central black hole, about 4 billion Suns, blasts out jets of particles at nearly the speed of light. The jets swell into radio lobes nearly a million light-years across.

Good to know

  • It is one of the brightest radio sources in the sky.
  • The jets are invisible to the eye; the drawing shows what radio telescopes see.
  • Its host galaxy is about 1,000 times as massive as the Milky Way.
