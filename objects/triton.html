Triton

Moon of Neptune · 4.5 billion km from the Sun

How big is Triton?

Triton is about 2,707 km across.

See Triton in 3D, at true scale

Triton orbits Neptune backwards, which almost certainly means it did not form there: it is a captured object from the Kuiper Belt, seized by Neptune's gravity. Voyager 2 flew past in 1989 and found nitrogen geysers erupting from one of the coldest surfaces ever measured.

At a glance

Diameter2,707 km
Mass2.14 × 10²² kg
Diameter compared with Earth0.212 × Earth
OrbitsNeptune, backwards, every 5.9 days
Surface−235 °C
AtmosphereThin nitrogen
Discovered1846, 17 days after Neptune

Good to know

  • Its retrograde orbit is slowly decaying; in a few billion years Neptune's tides will tear it into a ring.
  • Geysers throw nitrogen gas and dust 8 km up, where winds smear them into dark streaks.
  • Only about 40% of its surface was mapped by Voyager 2. No spacecraft has been back.
