VY Canis Majoris

Red hypergiant · 3,900 light-years

How big is VY Canis Majoris?

VY Canis Majoris is about 2.0 × 10⁹ km across.

See VY Canis Majoris in 3D, at true scale

VY Canis Majoris is a red hypergiant losing mass at a spectacular rate, wrapped in a nebula of its own making. It is among the largest stars known and one of the most luminous in our galaxy.

At a glance

Diameter2.0 × 10⁹ km
Mass3.38 × 10³¹ kg
Diameter compared with Earth155,000 × Earth
Distance3,900 light-years
Surface3,490 K
RadiusAround 1,400 Suns
Mass loss~30 Earth masses a year

Good to know

  • Placed at the Sun's position, its surface would extend past Jupiter's orbit.
  • It is so unstable that it throws off arcs and knots of material, mapped in detail by Hubble.
  • It is expected to collapse into a black hole rather than leave a neutron star.
