Europa

Moon of Jupiter · 778 million km from the Sun

How big is Europa?

Europa is about 3,122 km across.

See Europa in 3D, at true scale

Beneath Europa's cracked shell of ice lies an ocean holding perhaps twice as much water as every ocean on Earth combined. Jupiter's tides keep it liquid, and the seafloor may be warm. It is one of the best places we know to look for life beyond Earth.

At a glance

Diameter3,122 km
Mass4.80 × 10²² kg
Diameter compared with Earth0.245 × Earth
OrbitsJupiter, every 3.5 days
Ice shell15–25 km thick
Ocean60–150 km deep
Discovered1610, Galileo

Good to know

  • Its surface is the smoothest of any solid body in the Solar System, and has very few craters — the ice is being resurfaced.
  • NASA's Europa Clipper launched in October 2024 and will make dozens of close flybys from 2030.
  • The reddish streaks may be salts from the ocean below, darkened by Jupiter's radiation.
