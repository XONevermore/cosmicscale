R136a1

Wolf–Rayet star · 163,000 light-years

How big is R136a1?

R136a1 is about 5.9 × 10⁷ km across.

See R136a1 in 3D, at true scale

R136a1 is the most massive star known: around 200 Suns packed into a Wolf–Rayet star in the Tarantula Nebula. It is so luminous that it would outshine our Sun by millions of times.

At a glance

Diameter5.9 × 10⁷ km
Mass3.90 × 10³² kg
Diameter compared with Earth4,660 × Earth
Distance163,000 light-years
Brightness4.6 million Suns
Age1–2 million years

Good to know

  • It loses more mass in a year than the Sun does in a million.
  • Sharper images in 2022 lowered the mass estimate — crowded fields can make several stars look like one.
  • Stars this heavy live only a few million years before collapsing.
