Boomerang Nebula

Pre-planetary nebula · About 5,000 light-years

How big is Boomerang Nebula?

Boomerang Nebula is about 0.32 light-years across.

See Boomerang Nebula in 3D, at true scale

The Boomerang Nebula is a dying star in Centaurus blowing off its gas very fast. The light we see is the star's light reflected by dust in two narrow lobes, while its dusty waist hides the star itself.

At a glance

Diameter0.32 light-years
Diameter compared with Earth2.4 × 10⁸ × Earth
TemperatureAbout 1 K (−272 °C)
Gas speedAbout 164 km/s
DistanceAbout 5,000 light-years
ConstellationCentaurus

Good to know

  • It is colder than the empty space around it, which the Big Bang's afterglow keeps at 2.7 K.
  • It cools the way spray from an aerosol can feels cold: gas expanding very fast.
  • The ALMA telescope showed a narrow hourglass inside a huge, frigid, roughly round cloud.
