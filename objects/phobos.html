Phobos

Moon of Mars · 9,376 km from Mars

How big is Phobos?

Phobos is about 22.2 km across.

See Phobos in 3D, at true scale

Phobos is the larger moon of Mars and orbits closer to its planet than any other moon in the Solar System, only 6,000 km above the surface. It circles faster than Mars turns, so from the ground it rises in the west, twice a day.

At a glance

Diameter22.2 km
Mass1.06 × 10¹⁶ kg
Diameter compared with Earth0.002 × Earth
SizeAbout 27 × 22 × 18 km
OrbitsMars, every 7.7 hours
Discovered1877, Asaph Hall
Big craterStickney, 9 km wide

Good to know

  • It is spiralling inward by about 2 m every century and will break into a ring or crash in roughly 50 million years.
  • Long grooves across it may be stretch marks from Mars's pull.
  • Japan's MMX mission plans to bring a sample of it back to Earth.
