Vesta

Protoplanet in the asteroid belt · Asteroid belt

How big is Vesta?

Vesta is about 525 km across.

See Vesta in 3D, at true scale

Vesta is the second-largest body in the asteroid belt and the brightest asteroid in our sky. It is a surviving protoplanet: it melted and separated into a metal core and rocky crust, like a small planet that never finished growing.

At a glance

Diameter525 km
Mass2.59 × 10²⁰ kg
Diameter compared with Earth0.041 × Earth
SizeAbout 525 km
Orbit3.6 years
VisitedDawn, 2011–2012
Big craterRheasilvia, 500 km wide

Good to know

  • The central peak of Rheasilvia is about 22 km high, one of the tallest mountains in the Solar System.
  • About 1 in 20 meteorites that fall on Earth came from Vesta.
  • It is the only asteroid sometimes bright enough to see with the naked eye.
  • Next smaller: Enceladus · 504 km
  • Next larger: Ceres · 939 km
