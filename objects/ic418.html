IC 418

Planetary nebula · About 4,300 light-years

How big is IC 418?

IC 418 is about 0.26 light-years across.

See IC 418 in 3D, at true scale

IC 418 is the outer layers of a red giant star, thrown off only a few thousand years ago. The star's core is still shrinking and heating up on its way to becoming a white dwarf. Fine wavy lines across the gas gave it its Spirograph nickname.

Good to know

  • Nobody knows exactly what draws its fine criss-cross pattern of lines.
  • Its star will shrink to a white dwarf about the size of Earth.
  • Its gas flows out faster at the ends of the egg than at its middle.
