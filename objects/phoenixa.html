Phoenix A*

Ultramassive black hole · 5.8 billion light-years

How big is Phoenix A*?

Phoenix A* is about 0.06 light-years across.

See Phoenix A* in 3D, at true scale

Phoenix A* is the black hole at the heart of the Phoenix Cluster, and current estimates put it near 100 billion Suns — possibly the most massive known. Those estimates are indirect and uncertain, so treat the number with care.

At a glance

Diameter0.06 light-years
Mass1.99 × 10⁴¹ kg
Diameter compared with Earth4.6 × 10⁷ × Earth
Distance5.8 billion light-years
ClusterPhoenix Cluster
CertaintyIndirect estimate

Good to know

  • Its cluster forms stars at hundreds of times the usual rate for such systems.
  • The mass comes from scaling relations, not from watching stars orbit, so it could be considerably lower.
  • If the estimate holds, its event horizon would be as wide as about 65 Solar Systems laid end to end.
