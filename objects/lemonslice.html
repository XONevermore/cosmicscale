Lemon Slice Nebula

Round planetary nebula (IC 3568) · About 7,000 light-years

How big is Lemon Slice Nebula?

Lemon Slice Nebula is about 0.60 light-years across.

See Lemon Slice Nebula in 3D, at true scale

The Lemon Slice, IC 3568, is a planetary nebula with a bright inner shell in a smooth outer shell, around a star of over 50,000 degrees. Sources disagree badly on its distance; the Gaia spacecraft's reading of about 7,000 light-years is the one used here.

Good to know

  • Robert Aitken was following a comet when he saw that one catalogued star was really a tiny nebula.
  • Its yellow lemon colour is not real: it comes from how the Hubble image was coloured.
  • It was once mistaken for a compact galaxy.
