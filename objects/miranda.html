Miranda

Moon of Uranus · 129,390 km from Uranus

How big is Miranda?

Miranda is about 472 km across.

See Miranda in 3D, at true scale

Miranda is the smallest of Uranus's five large moons and one of the strangest-looking worlds known: a patchwork of grooved terrain, giant cliffs and smooth plains, as if it had been shattered and pieced back together.

Good to know

  • Verona Rupes, a cliff about 20 km high, may be the tallest in the Solar System.
  • A rock dropped from the top would take about 12 minutes to fall.
  • Only the southern half has ever been photographed.
