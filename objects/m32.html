Messier 32

Compact elliptical galaxy · 2.5 million light-years

How big is Messier 32?

Messier 32 is about 6,600 light-years across.

See Messier 32 in 3D, at true scale

Messier 32 is a compact elliptical galaxy beside Andromeda, the prototype of its kind. Guillaume Le Gentil found it in 1749, 15 years before Messier listed it. Half of its light comes from within about 360 light-years of its centre.

Good to know

  • It packs hundreds of millions of Suns of stars into a core only a few hundred light-years wide.
  • It may be what is left of a galaxy of about 25 billion Suns of stars that Andromeda tore apart about 2 billion years ago.
  • Its centre hides a black hole of about 2.5 million Suns, over half the Milky Way's.
