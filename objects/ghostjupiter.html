Ghost of Jupiter Nebula

Planetary nebula (NGC 3242) · About 4,200 light-years

How big is Ghost of Jupiter Nebula?

Ghost of Jupiter Nebula is about 0.94 light-years across.

See Ghost of Jupiter Nebula in 3D, at true scale

The Ghost of Jupiter is a planetary nebula in Hydra: gas thrown off by a dying star, lit by its hot exposed core. A bright oval inner shell sits inside a fainter outer one, about a light-year across. William Herschel found it in 1785.

At a glance

DistanceAbout 4,200 light-years

ConstellationHydra

Good to know

  • Its central star is more than ten times hotter than the Sun's surface.
  • Two red knots sit at the ends of its inner shell.
  • Deep images show faint rings around it, puffs the star threw off thousands of years earlier.
