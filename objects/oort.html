Oort Cloud

Shell of icy bodies around the Sun · Starts ~2,000 AU from the Sun

How big is Oort Cloud?

Oort Cloud is about 3.16 light-years across.

See Oort Cloud in 3D, at true scale

The Oort Cloud is a vast shell of icy bodies surrounding the Solar System, so far out that passing stars can nudge its members loose. Nobody has ever seen it directly — we infer it from the comets that fall inward from every direction.

At a glance

Diameter3.16 light-years
Diameter compared with Earth2.3 × 10⁹ × Earth
Inner edgeAbout 2,000 AU
Outer edge50,000–100,000 AU
Proposed1950, Jan Oort
MembersPerhaps trillions

Good to know

  • Its outer edge is roughly a third of the way to Proxima Centauri.
  • A comet falling in from there can take millions of years to reach the inner Solar System.
  • Voyager 1 will need about 300 years just to reach the inner edge.
