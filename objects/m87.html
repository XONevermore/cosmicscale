M87*

Supermassive black hole · 53 million light-years

How big is M87*?

M87* is about 3.8 × 10¹⁰ km across.

See M87* in 3D, at true scale

M87* was the first black hole ever photographed. In 2019 a network of radio telescopes spanning the planet produced the now-famous orange ring — the shadow of an object 6.5 billion times the Sun's mass, 53 million light-years away.

Good to know

  • Its event horizon is wider than our entire Solar System, including the Kuiper Belt.
  • A jet of plasma blasts out of it at nearly the speed of light and is visible in ordinary telescopes.
  • The image needed so much data that the hard drives were flown, not transmitted, to be combined.
