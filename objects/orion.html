Orion Nebula

Star-forming nebula · 1,344 light-years

How big is Orion Nebula?

Orion Nebula is about 24.0 light-years across.

See Orion Nebula in 3D, at true scale

The Orion Nebula is the nearest large stellar nursery, a glowing cavern of gas being carved out by the young stars inside it. It is the fuzzy middle 'star' in Orion's sword, visible to the naked eye.

At a glance

Diameter24.0 light-years
Mass3.98 × 10³³ kg
Diameter compared with Earth1.8 × 10¹⁰ × Earth
Distance1,344 light-years
AgeUnder 3 million years
Also known asM42

Good to know

  • Four hot young stars called the Trapezium light the whole nebula.
  • Hubble found hundreds of protoplanetary disks here — solar systems in the act of forming.
  • It is only the bright face of a much larger cloud that covers most of the constellation.
