Hourglass Nebula

Planetary nebula · About 10,000 light-years

How big is Hourglass Nebula?

Hourglass Nebula is about 1.00 light-years across.

See Hourglass Nebula in 3D, at true scale

The Hourglass Nebula is gas thrown off by a dying star, pinched at the waist into an hourglass. A thick ring of gas around the star's middle forced its winds out towards the top and bottom. Its walls are etched with rings like the grooves of a vase.

Good to know

  • Its central star sits a little off-centre, likely tugged by a hidden companion star.
  • Small knots of gas are flying out of it at about 500 km every second.
  • The "eye" in the middle is a smaller hourglass seen inside the big one.
