Porphyrion

Largest known black-hole jets · 7.5 billion light-years

How big is Porphyrion?

Porphyrion is about 23,000,000 light-years across.

See Porphyrion in 3D, at true scale

Porphyrion is a pair of jets from a supermassive black hole that stretch 23 million light-years end to end — the largest black-hole jets ever seen, found in 2024 with the LOFAR radio telescope.

Named afterA giant of Greek myth

Good to know

  • The jets would reach across 140 Milky Ways lined up end to end.
  • They were blasted out when the universe was about 6 billion years old.
  • Jets like this may have spread magnetism through the space between galaxies.
