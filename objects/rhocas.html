Rho Cassiopeiae

Yellow hypergiant · About 3,400 light-years

How big is Rho Cassiopeiae?

Rho Cassiopeiae is about 8.8 × 10⁸ km across.

See Rho Cassiopeiae in 3D, at true scale

Rho Cassiopeiae is a yellow hypergiant, one of the rarest kinds of star, about half a million times brighter than the Sun; in the Sun's place it would reach into the asteroid belt. It is visible to the naked eye despite being thousands of light-years away.

Good to know

  • In 2000 it shed about 3% of the Sun's mass in a single outburst.
  • Only a handful of yellow hypergiants are known in the whole Milky Way.
  • It is unstable and pulsates, dimming and brightening irregularly.
