XTE J1650-500

Small stellar black hole · About 8,500 light-years

How big is XTE J1650-500?

XTE J1650-500 is about 29.5 km across.

See XTE J1650-500 in 3D, at true scale

XTE J1650-500 is a black hole in Ara, found when it flared in X-rays in 2001. Its mass is only bounded, so the size shown is for 5 Suns. This view is an illustration.

At a glance

Diameter29.5 km
Mass9.94 × 10³⁰ kg
Diameter compared with Earth0.002 × Earth
Companion's orbit7.7 hours
CompanionA small orange star
DistanceAbout 8,500 light-years

Good to know

  • Its companion star whips around it every 7.7 hours.
  • In 2008 it was announced as the smallest black hole known, at 3.8 Suns; better limits say only about 4 to 7.
  • Its X-rays flicker 250 times a second, from gas very close to the black hole.
