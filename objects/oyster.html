Oyster Nebula

Planetary nebula · About 5,400 light-years

How big is Oyster Nebula?

Oyster Nebula is about 1.14 light-years across.

See Oyster Nebula in 3D, at true scale

The Oyster Nebula is a shell of gas blown off by a dying star. The bright "pearl" at its centre is the star's exposed core, one of the hottest kinds of star known. Its surface is covered in a tangled web of glowing gas.

At a glance

Also known asNGC 1501

Good to know

  • Its central star is about 20 times hotter than the Sun's surface.
  • The star flickers in brightness every 20 to 50 minutes as waves ring through it.
  • The shell is swelling by about 40 to 55 km every second.
