Vega

White main-sequence star · 25 light-years

How big is Vega?

Vega is about 3,284,000 km across.

See Vega in 3D, at true scale

Vega was the first star ever photographed and the first to have its spectrum recorded. It spins so fast that it bulges at the equator and is noticeably cooler there than at its poles.

At a glance

Diameter3,284,000 km
Mass4.26 × 10³⁰ kg
Diameter compared with Earth258 × Earth
Distance25 light-years
Surface9,600 K
Spin236 km/s at the equator
Age455 million years

Good to know

  • It was the northern pole star around 12,000 BC, and will be again around AD 13,700.
  • A disk of dust surrounds it — the first such debris disk ever found, and a sign of planet formation.
  • For a century it was the reference point for measuring the brightness of every other star.

  • Next larger: S2 · 8,488,000 km
