Alpha Centauri B

Orange dwarf · 4.37 light-years

How big is Alpha Centauri B?

Alpha Centauri B is about 1,201,000 km across.

See Alpha Centauri B in 3D, at true scale

Alpha Centauri B is the slightly smaller, cooler partner in the closest star system to the Sun. Together with Alpha Centauri A it forms a bright pair that looks like a single star to the naked eye, with Proxima orbiting far out.

At a glance

Diameter1,201,000 km
Mass1.81 × 10³⁰ kg
Diameter compared with Earth94.3 × Earth
Distance4.37 light-years
Surface5,260 K
Orbit80 years around its twin
TypeOrange dwarf

Good to know

  • The two stars swing between 11 and 36 times the Earth–Sun distance apart.
  • Alpha Centauri is the third-brightest star in the night sky, but is only visible from below about 29° north.
  • Breakthrough Starshot proposes firing gram-scale probes here with lasers, arriving in about 20 years.
