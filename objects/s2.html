S2

Star orbiting Sagittarius A* · 26,700 light-years

How big is S2?

S2 is about 8,488,000 km across.

See S2 in 3D, at true scale

S2 is a hot young star orbiting Sagittarius A*, the black hole at the centre of the Milky Way. Following it around its 16-year orbit for three decades proved that something four million times the Sun's mass hides there, and won the 2020 Nobel Prize in Physics.

Good to know

  • In 2018 its light was stretched by the black hole's gravity exactly as Einstein predicted.
  • Its whole orbit slowly turns with each lap, another effect of relativity, first measured in 2020.
  • It is about 14 times the Sun's mass, yet it is whipped around like a pebble on a string.
